01 · CONCEPT

Mirni stol, glasne karte.

A calm table, loud cards.

The app is the table: quiet, neutral, legible and out of the way. Menus, setup, lobby, dictionaries, voting and settings all live here. The only things allowed to be loud are the cards — your role, an elimination, the final reveal. The mischief lives in the cards, never in the chrome.

PERSONALITY Confident — big type, one decisive action per screen. Mischievous — the spy card is the brand. Discreet — a secret only ever lives on a card face.

FOUR RULES
Vermilion means spy. Nowhere else, except the brand mark.
One primary action per screen, always at the thumb.
Only cards cast shadows. The table is flat.
Secrets live on card faces — never in titles, toasts or the app switcher.
