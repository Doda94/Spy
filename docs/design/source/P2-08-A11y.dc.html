SCREEN READER · VoiceOver / TalkBack labels
{{ v.s }} {{ v.l }}
Privacy with a screen reader on: before the first reveal, ask once — "Uloga će se izgovoriti naglas. Stavi slušalice." — and offer to show the role silently with large text instead. Then announce once; never re-read on focus.
Targets 48 pt/dp minimum for anything tapped during play; 8 pt between neighbours. Primary actions sit in the bottom third. The whole card is the flip target, not just the button.
Text to 200% Body and labels scale fully. Rows reflow: steppers drop under their label; chip rows wrap to 2 lines. Display sizes cap at 1.3× and rely on auto-fit; the card grows to full width.
Transparency & contrast Reduce Transparency: glass chrome → solid surface-2 with a 1 pt line. Increase Contrast: line → n400, muted text → n700, card frame stroke 2 pt.
Countdown speech Polite live region at each full minute, at 30 s, 10 s and 0 — never every second. On focus it reads "Preostalo 4 minute i 32 sekunde".
