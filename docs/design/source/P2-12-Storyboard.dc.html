12 · TABLE CHOREOGRAPHY · try it in board 11

The table is the camera.

Every player has a face-down card on a round table. Whoever's turn it is, the table turns their seat towards the phone holder, the camera pushes in, and their card lifts into their hands. Between turns the camera pulls back and the table turns to the next seat — a visible "pass the phone". During discussion the table stays on screen, cards breathing, and it gets more nervous as time runs out.

IDLE TENSION · cards on the table during discussion
Calm · > 30 sEach card drifts ±1.4° and 4 pt up, 3.2–4.6 s per card, phases offset so they never move in unison. The table turns once every 90 s.
Uneasy · ≤ 30 sDrift speeds up to 1.3–1.9 s. Table edge and digits turn amber; one warning haptic.
Nervous · ≤ 10 sCards tremble (±1 pt, 0.26 s). Digits pulse 1.0 → 1.07 each second.
Time up · 0Everything freezes, the edge flashes twice, alarm haptic. In-app voting opens; out-loud mode waits for the host.
Same table, whole game The cards on the table are the "who's still in" list. A knock-out flicks that card off the table, leaving a faint empty seat; tap a seat to see the name. At game over every card flips face-up where it sits, spies first.
Each on their own phone Same table on every device, but your seat is always at the bottom — no turning, the camera only pushes in on your own card. Other seats get a small neutral tick when that player has looked; roles never travel to other phones.
Reduce Motion & build notes Reduced: no table turn, drift or tremble; the current seat gets an ivory ring and the card cross-fades in. Compose: table = graphicsLayer rotationZ + scale on animateFloatAsState; lift = shared element (SharedTransitionLayout); idle = rememberInfiniteTransition per card; flip = rotationY + cameraDistance. No shaders.
